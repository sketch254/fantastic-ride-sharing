Questions before you ride

How do I request a ride?

Open "Find a Ride," pick a departure, expand its details, and select "Request to join." The driver reviews and responds to your request.

How does payment work?

Ride-My-Way doesn't process payments. Drivers and passengers settle up directly, usually in cash at pickup.

What's expected of riders and drivers?

Show up on time for confirmed rides, keep ride details accurate, and treat everyone in the car with respect.
